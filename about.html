The Walker Way

We operate on the four pillars of Maggie L. Walker Governor's School:

Courage

Tackling hard problems, shipping imperfect code, and learning from every bug.

Compassion

Helping teammates debug, mentoring newcomers, and building inclusive tech.

Community

One room, one whiteboard, one repo — we grow stronger together.

Collaboration

Pair programming, code reviews, and group projects that ship.
