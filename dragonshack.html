DragonsHack

MLWGS Programming Club’s annual hackathon. 24 hours. One weekend. Build something real.

• Coming Soon • Late January – Early February

What to Expect

Build

Form a team, pick a problem, and ship a working prototype in 24 hours.

Learn

Workshops and mentors available throughout the event for all skill levels.

Compete

Demo your project to judges. Prizes for best overall, best design, and best beginner hack.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate? +
Any MLWGS student is welcome! No prior experience required — we have mentors and workshops for beginners.
Do I need a team? +
You can come solo or with a team. We'll help you find teammates at the event if needed.
What should I bring? +
Just your laptop and charger. We'll provide food, drinks, Wi-Fi, and swag.
Is it free? +
Yes! DragonsHack is completely free for all MLWGS students.
When is it exactly? +
Late January to early February. Exact dates coming soon — check back or get notified!
